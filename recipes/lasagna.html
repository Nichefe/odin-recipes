Lasagna

Exquisite Lasagne to share with friends and family, easy to cook

A love focused recipe that has been passed down through generations, and now we wish to share with you.

Famously aclaimed as the best lasagna in the world it has gathered titles oevr more than 7 consecutive years.

Ingredients

  • Meat
  • An onion and two cloves of garlic
  • A can of crushed tomatoes, two cans of tomato sauce, and two cans of tomato paste
  • Two tablespoons of white sugar
  • Fresh parsley, dried basil leaves, salt, Italian seasoning, fennel seeds, and black pepper
  • Lasagna noodles
  • Parmesan, mozzarella, and ricotta cheese
  • An egg
